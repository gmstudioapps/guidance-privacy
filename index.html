Privacy Policy for Guidance

Effective date: [[EFFECTIVE DATE]]
App: Guidance (Android) — package com.gmstudio.guidance
Published by: GM Studio

The short version. Guidance does not collect your personal data. There is no account to create, no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking of any kind. Everything the app remembers — where you are up to in your reading, the verses you have saved, your reminder times and your settings — is stored only on your own phone. None of it is ever sent to us, and we have no way to see it.

The app works fully offline. The complete Quran is included inside the app itself.

1. What Guidance stores, and where

The app stores the following on your device, in storage that is private to the app:

  • Your reading position and progress — the verse you are up to, the verses you have read in sequence, and the daily reading pace you have chosen.
  • Your favourites — verses you have saved, the collections you have organised them into, and a copy of the wording of each saved verse as it read when you saved it.
  • Your reminders — whether reminders are on, and the times of day you chose.
  • Your settings — your chosen English translation, your colour theme, whether the app follows your phone's light/dark setting, and how your home-screen widget is set up (what it shows, its colours, and whether it has a background card).
  • A small technical marker used to tell a brand-new install from one that has been restored from a backup, so the app can re-arm your reminders correctly.
  • The date you last used "Refresh offline Quran", if you have used it.

These are stored in Android's standard app preferences file (roughly 20 KB in total). If you use "Refresh offline Quran", a fresh copy of the Quran text (about 3 MB) is also saved in the app's own private file storage.

What Guidance does not collect at all: your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, files, calendar, microphone or camera input, device or advertising identifiers, or any usage or behavioural data. The app has no analytics, no crash-reporting service, and no advertising software in it.

2. No account, and no data sent to us

Guidance has no sign-up, no login and no user accounts. Because nothing you do in the app is transmitted to the developer or to any server, there is no copy of your data anywhere for us to read, store, share or sell. We do not have access to your reading progress, your favourites, your reminders or your settings.

3. Backup to your own Google account

Guidance uses Android's built-in Auto Backup, the standard feature that lets your app data survive changing or resetting your phone. This means:

  • A copy of the settings file described in Section 1 (roughly 20 KB — reading progress, favourites, reminder times and settings) is backed up to your own Google account / Google Drive, if you have Android's backup feature switched on.
  • This backup belongs to you, not to us. It is stored under your Google account, it does not count against your Drive storage quota, and the developer of this app cannot access it. Its handling is governed by Google's own terms and privacy policy.
  • The same data may be copied directly from your old phone to your new one during Android's phone-to-phone setup transfer.
  • Deliberately not backed up: the downloaded offline Quran copy (the app already contains it), the home-screen widget's temporary display data, and the scheduled-notification queue (which the app rebuilds from your reminder times).

You control this. It can be switched off in Android's own settings — typically Settings → Google → Backup — either for the whole phone or for individual apps, depending on your device.

4. When the app uses the internet

Guidance is designed to work with no internet connection at all. The entire Quran — the Arabic text and both English translations — is included inside the app when you install it, so reading, Today's Verse, browsing chapters, reminders and the widget all work offline.

The app connects to the internet in only these situations:

  • "Refresh offline Quran" (More → Offline data) — and only when you tap it yourself. This downloads a fresh copy of the Quran text from the public alquran.cloud service and replaces the copy stored on your device. The request contains no personal information and no identifier of you or your device. As with any internet request, the service you are connecting to will necessarily see your device's IP address and standard connection information; that handling is governed by alquran.cloud's own terms and privacy practices, not ours.
  • Opening the tanzil.net link on the About & Credits screen — this hands off to your phone's web browser and you leave the app. What happens then is between you, your browser and that site.

Nothing else in the app makes a network request.

5. Permissions the app asks for, and why

  • Internet — used only for the manual "Refresh offline Quran" action described above.
  • Run at startup — so your reminders are still scheduled after you restart your phone. Without it, restarting silently cancels them.
  • Notifications — to show the verse reminders you have set up. Android asks your permission the first time; if you decline, everything else in the app still works normally.

Guidance deliberately does not request location, contacts, storage, camera, microphone, or exact-alarm permissions.

If you choose the "Match my Wallpaper" option for the home-screen widget's text colour, the app asks Android for the dominant colours of your current wallpaper. It receives colour values only — it does not read, copy or store your wallpaper image, and those colours are not sent anywhere.

6. Third-party software in the app

Guidance is built with Flutter and uses a small number of standard open-source components: for storing settings on the device, saving a file to the device, showing local notifications, drawing the home-screen widget, picking colours, displaying artwork, reading the app's version number, following your phone's theme colours, opening a web link in your browser, handling time zones for reminders, and making the one network request described in Section 4.

None of these components collect personal data, show advertising, or report analytics. There is no advertising network, analytics service, crash-reporting service or tracking software of any kind in this app.

7. Children

Guidance is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. Nothing in the app is unsuitable for a younger reader — there is no advertising, no in-app purchases, and no user-generated content or communication features of any kind — but the app is not designed, marketed or listed as a children's app.

Guidance collects no personal information from anyone, of any age, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; since the app collects no personal information at all, there is nothing held about any user for us to review or delete.

8. How to delete your data

  • Everything on your phone: uninstalling Guidance permanently deletes all of the app's data from your device — your reading progress, favourites, reminders and settings. You can also use Android's Settings → Apps → Guidance → Storage → Clear data to reset the app without uninstalling it.
  • The backup copy in your Google account is separate and survives uninstalling. To remove it, go to your Google Drive backups (typically Settings → Google → Backup, or drive.google.com under "Backups"), find Guidance and delete its backup data — or switch backup off for the app so no further copies are made.

Because no data ever reaches us, there is nothing for us to delete on our side, and no deletion request needs to be sent to us.

9. How your data is kept safe

App data on Android is stored in a private area that other apps on the phone cannot read. Nothing you do in the app is transmitted, so there is no data in transit to be intercepted. The optional Google backup is encrypted and secured by Google under your own account.
